


After deploying to render.com, we need to add its rewrite rule as in 
SPA routes are not physically located in server and js will redirect from
index.html

below are the steps:

In your Render service (laughing-enigma), go to the left sidebar under Manage → click Redirects/Rewrites.
Click Add Rule.
Set:
Source: /*
Destination: /index.html
Action: Rewrite
Click Save.
