Suggested implementation sequence:

Phase 1 — Idempotency

Implement this first.
messageId / registrationId
        ↓
deduplication
        ↓
business operation

Phase 2 — Retry

Add:
3–4 attempts
exponential backoff

and classify retryable vs non-retryable exceptions.

Phase 3 — DLQ

Create dedicated DLQs and make sure failed messages retain enough information for investigation/reprocessing.

Phase 4 — Backpressure

Tune:
concurrency
max-concurrency
prefetch

based on the capacity of each service/database.

Phase 5 — Observability

Add metrics such as:
rabbitmq_messages_consumed
rabbitmq_messages_failed
rabbitmq_retry_count
rabbitmq_dlq_messages
rabbitmq_consumer_latency
rabbitmq_unacked_messages

Then you can actually demonstrate:
"The system retries transient failures, isolates poison messages in DLQs, and limits consumer pressure through controlled concurrency and prefetch."

That would make a very solid production-engineering addition to your registration saga, rather than merely adding RabbitMQ features for the sake of it.
